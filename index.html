    How to play

    Each player secretly places a fleet on a 10×10 grid (rows A–J, columns 1–10). Ships go straight across or straight down, never diagonally, never off the grid, never on top of each other.

    Take turns calling one square, like D-4. The other player answers miss or hit, and on a hit says which ship. When every square of a ship is hit, its owner announces it sank. Sink all five ships first to win.

    Salvo

    You call one shot for each of your ships still afloat: five to start, one fewer for each of your ships that has been sunk. The current box has you call them one at a time, hearing each answer; the 1967 box has you call them all at once, then hear the answers.

    Other switches

    Don’t name the ship (the box’s harder Salvo game): a hit is just “hit”; sinkings are still announced.
    Ships can’t touch (a common house rule): no two ships side by side or corner to corner.
